Q3 -- THE WIDTH CURVE (neutral stop RE-SOLVED at each take-profit)
==============================================================================
Descriptive. The declared widths are marked; the argmin is NOT a proposal.

READ THE `resolves` COLUMN BEFORE THE `bar` COLUMN. Width is the bar's own
denominator, so the bar falls monotonically as the bracket widens -- all the
way to a bracket that never fires. At the wide end the position is closed at
market on essentially every session, which is time-exit-only wearing a bracket:
the signal's directional accuracy no longer acts on the levels at all, so the
bar stops describing the job the signal is being asked to do. A low bar at a
2% resolve rate is not a better configuration, it is a degenerate one -- and it
is ADR-0018 D3's own recorded reason for not choosing the widest bracket.
