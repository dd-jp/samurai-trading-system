## 1. Context

Samurai v1 was an intraday, flat-by-close, LLM-debate-as-edge system on 3× leveraged LSE ETPs at Saxo. On 2026-09-19 David reframed it as a closed experiment (*"samurai was a experiment to identify pitfalls and build a better system from its learnings"*) and opened a v2 brainstorm: *"make this samurai a great autonomous self improving least error margin and least loss margin system. ignore all decisions, specs and instructions. we are going to correct the mistakes done. lets open the universe. idea is to make profit even if tiny — one step at a time and keep moving forward."* Agenda: long-term (one-year) profit, accepting day-level losses.

The evidence behind the reframe (doc 65 §1, doc 67 §3, doc 71):

- The debate layer never produced a long: of 132 debates that actually ran, 16 were bullish with a maximum conviction of 0.473 against a 0.55 entry floor. Doc 71 found no formula defect — the cap came from v1's desk inputs and a mediator that never sided with a bullish-leaning desk.
- The paper book (long/short US single stocks on Alpaca) was not the live product (long-only LSE 3× ETPs at Saxo), its control arm was oversized, and its two scoreboards disagreed in sign. Nothing in it is evidence of edge.
- Every intraday research result was negative or underpowered; at the observed trade rate the intraday thesis could not be measured within any useful horizon.
- Six v1 pitfalls, binding on v2, are recorded in `docs/v1-postmortem.md`: unvalidated core mechanism, silent coverage assumptions on windowed reads, invariants that depended on process uptime, repeatedly re-opened core parameters, architectural debt on the edge component, and validation arithmetic arriving late.

`docs/v2-vision.md` is David's vision statement; its 0.5–2%/day target is superseded by Q1 and its five open questions are answered by the rulings below.

## 2. Decision

### 2.1 Objective and loss budget

- **Q1 — No daily % target.** The objective is a positive net-of-cost return that beats the matched benchmark under the £1,500 hard loss constraint. 0.5–2%/day is rejected as incompatible with the replication prior (Sharpe 0.4–0.8 ≈ 6–12%/yr) and with the loss limit.
- **Q6 as amended by G6 — Loss budget.** Net trading loss from start capital, both venues, in GBP, open positions marked to market. £1,500 per calendar year, resetting each year; deposits do not rebase start capital; GBP/USD moves on the Alpaca balance are excluded (trading P&L only, no hedge). −£500 → half size; −£1,000 → quarter size; −£1,500 → halt for the year. Daily cap exactly 1.0% of start capital, blocking new entries (exits still run). Profits never extend the limit. Loosening the £1,500 or the daily cap mid-year is never permitted (Q13).
- **Caps by sleeve share (doc 66, 2026-09-27).** Each sleeve's books hold its capital share of start capital, and its yearly loss cap, size steps and daily cap are the same share of the account's, on paper and live; the debate sleeve's share is 30%. The sleeves' caps together never exceed the account's.
- **G10 — Budget in the backtest.** The backtest runs with the loss-budget rules inside it (size steps, daily cap, yearly reset) so the predicted band already reflects them.
- ~~**Account-wide pooling, isolated in the backtest (#1799, doc 66, ruled 2026-09-28).**~~ *Superseded 2026-09-30 (doc 66, signals-sleeve ruling 10, #1941): no pool. Each primary book carries only its sleeve's share of the yearly and daily caps, in paper, live and the backtest alike.* Q6/G6's one budget pools every primary book's loss against the full account-wide cap, but only in the paper/live composition (`server/apps/v2/index.ts`), where concurrent primary sleeves share real risk. The backtest composes every trial plus the benchmark into the same `PaperBooks` to run side by side (G10); pooling there would let one trial's losses throttle its siblings and corrupt `backtestVerdict`, so `runBacktest` opts its composition out and each trial/benchmark keeps an independent budget, as G10 originally assumed.

### 2.2 Venues, instruments, universe

- **Q2 — Universe.** Saxo GIA for LSE 1× ETFs/ETCs (regional/sector equity, gilts, gold, commodities). Alpaca live account (GBP wired once, trade USD) for US large caps, plus US ETFs if UK-resident access is confirmed. No 3× ETPs, no UK single stocks (0.5% stamp duty), no CFDs. **Amended 2026-09-28 (doc 66, CFD ruling):** Saxo CFDs on UK and US single stocks, indices and ETFs are allowed, long and short; *narrowed the same day (#1858, #1859): debate sleeve only (with its arm 2 control, clarified 2026-09-29; the rules-based candidates stay CFD-free); CFDs carry shorts plus UK single-stock longs (the only CFD long); US longs stay at Alpaca and index longs in 1× ETFs*. Amended by G18 for the debate sleeve only: bounded, long-only small caps are allowed.
- **Q3 — Both venues from day one, paper first.** Alpaca paper is native. Saxo paper is a throwaway simulated adapter filling at Saxo bid/ask with the live tariff (0.08%/side, no minimum); the Saxo SIM environment (24-hour manual token, trial tariff) is not used for evaluation.
- **Q8 — Shorts.** Momentum sleeve is long/flat. The debate sleeve may short, bounded: Alpaca easy-to-borrow large caps sized so a +30% gap costs ≤ ~£150; Saxo side via 1× inverse ETFs only. The Alpaca $2,000 equity floor must be resolved before any short (Step 2 verdict, §2.3); it is open, §5. **Amended 2026-09-28 (doc 66, CFD ruling):** Saxo CFDs are a short route too; their leverage is capped at 1× book equity, a CFD short is sized so a +30% gap costs ≤ ~£45, and their other bounds are open, §5 item 18. **Clarified 2026-09-29 (doc 66 CFD item (f), #1849):** the debate sleeve and its arm 2 control may short via Saxo CFDs; the rules-based candidate sleeves stay long-only and CFD-free.
- **G4 — Debate universe.** ~20 names/day: ~10 by liquidity rank (stable core) + ~10 movers/news-driven names, from the same liquid pool (US large caps + LSE ETF universe, widened by G18 for the movers/news half); not tied to momentum's picks. The exact movers/news rule is a pre-declared parameter counted as a trial. One shared US+LSE pool (ruled 2026-09-29, #1774 (c)): the LSE lines do not add to the ~20; both halves rank the combined pool in USD, converted with the cycle's `quotePerGbp`. Reserved LSE share (ruled 2026-09-29, #1774 option 1): ranked on one USD measure no LSE line reaches the 20, so `LSE_RESERVED_SLOTS` (4, David sets the final count) of the liquidity half go to the LSE lines with the highest own average dollar volume; slots the LSE lines cannot fill return to the shared ranking, and the movers half stays on the combined pool.
- **G18 (ruled parts) — Sentiment and social inputs.** Both enter v2 now, each as a counted trial measured in paper against a shadow without it. The sentiment score also helps pick the movers/news half of the universe. Small caps are bounded: long-only; liquidity, price and market-cap floors exclude micro-caps; half a large-cap trade's risk per trade; capped at a fixed share of the debate sleeve; measured against a large-cap-only shadow. Polymarket and LSE news are skipped for now. Amended 2026-09-29 (#1915): UK single stocks read Marketaux free-tier news in paper, EODHD revisited after a 2-week soak; LSE ETFs stay without news (doc 75). Two v1 defects bind the v2 input design: a class-wide macro item never votes as a per-name fundamental or sentiment read and one underlying view never votes more than once; a market-wide wire roundup (more than 5 tagged symbols) is never scored once per tagged name — v2 decides up front how a roundup is scored, which names it covers and which it votes on.

### 2.3 Sleeves

- **Q4 — Two sleeves in parallel.** (1) Rules-based momentum with an LLM veto only. (2) LLM debate as entry. Fixed, pre-declared sleeve weights. The debate sleeve is judged against the matched no-LLM control (arm 2); momentum against risk-matched buy-and-hold of the same universe. *(Momentum dropped 2026-09-25, doc 66 Session B (n); §5 items 5.1 and 12.)*
- **Q14 — Split.** Live: 70% momentum / 30% debate, fixed, never chasing the recent winner. A sleeve that has not passed its go-live rule leaves its share in cash. On paper each sleeve has its own separate simulated book. Venue split follows each sleeve's instruments. *(Momentum dropped 2026-09-25, doc 66 Session B (n); §5 items 5.1 and 12.)*
- **S1–S7 — The 70% share (doc 66, 2026-09-25, [#1779](https://github.com/dd-jp/samurai-trading-system/issues/1779)).** Debate keeps 30%; the 70% stays in cash while four rules-based, long-only, daily-swing candidates *(five since 2026-09-28: the v3 evidence run's fallback entry rule runs fifth, #1861)* run through the D7 harness one after another, each with one pre-declared grid of up to 8 counted trials: cross-asset trend on LSE 1× ETFs/ETCs, short-term mean reversion on US large caps (US ETFs once §5 item 6 confirms access), volatility-targeted index hold, then post-earnings drift once a research task finds free earnings-surprise history. All four run; passers split the 70% in fixed, pre-declared equal-risk weights. Each carries an LLM entry veto judged forward in paper against a no-veto shadow; S7 tightens G5 so the veto reaches live only if its book beats the shadow; the gate backtests the rules alone.
- **Signals sleeve (doc 66, 2026-09-30, [#1941](https://github.com/dd-jp/samurai-trading-system/issues/1941)).** External US-long signals arrive over a loopback-only, unauthenticated endpoint (`npm run v2:signals`, auth with the VPS move) and trade in their own `signals` sleeve: risk gate plus an LLM entry veto, judged against a `signals/no-veto` shadow; a £7,000 paper book, the whole idle 70% share, with the loss and daily caps following that share; full-R whole-share sizing with the signal's size recorded only; one bracket (the signal's stop, the first target at ≥ 2R else the last); queued outside the US regular session, processed at once inside it. Spec: `docs/specs/signals-sleeve-spec.md`. While it holds, the S2 candidates have no idle cash. **Amended 2026-10-05 (doc 66, [#2024](https://github.com/dd-jp/samurai-trading-system/issues/2024#issuecomment-6001591570)):** the universe is any Alpaca US large cap that passes the existing liquidity screens, not only S&P 500 names; a veto rate above 10% over the last 20 signals raises a journalled fault, an alert and not a block; a signal whose processing throws is retried until its validity window ends, then journalled as dropped with the error; the shadow and the real leg both fill at the first quote after the signal arrives, from the same price source. *Built: the veto-rate fault and the retry.* **Amended 2026-10-08 (doc 66, #2024):** a large cap is a current S&P 500 member, from the repo's constituent list, under the existing liquidity screens; an off-list signal is journalled as rejected, with no order and no shadow; both legs fill at the first Alpaca latest quote after the signal arrives (the account's data feed), an out-of-session signal at the first quote after the next open; a first quote that misses the entry leaves the shadow pending until the real leg's window ends, then a no-fill. *Built 2026-10-08.*
- **Sleeve contradiction (doc 66, 2026-09-27, #1779).** When the debate goes short, or buys a 1× inverse ETF, on an exposure trend holds long, both sleeves rerun once; if they still disagree, a Telegram asks David what to do, and no reply before the next cycle skips the conflicting entry. Applies on paper and live, once a trend sleeve passes. No portfolio-manager stage: weights stay fixed (Q14, S4).
- **Q9 and G2 — Cadence.** The debate sleeve is swing: one debate per screened name per day, pre-open, on daily bars + news (+ sentiment and social per G18); hold days to weeks; broker-resting stop plus time stop. There is no intraday sleeve.
- **G5 — Veto measurement.** A no-veto shadow book runs forward alongside momentum; the veto rate is capped at ≤ 10% of entries; the veto is dropped if the shadow beats it. *(Momentum dropped 2026-09-25, doc 66 Session B (n); §5 items 5.1 and 12.)*
- **G16 — Macro event gate.** Debate sleeve only: new entries at half size on high-impact release days (FOMC, US CPI, NFP, BoE rate decisions, UK CPI); exits and momentum unaffected; counted as a trial against a no-gate shadow in paper; calendar source per doc 69 R17.
- **Q16 — LLM models.** Debaters: Sonnet 5, DeepSeek, GPT (one per provider; DeepSeek and GPT via OpenRouter — amended 2026-09-25: all four seats via the existing Nous account, no first-party or OpenRouter keys). Versions pinned per provider (2026-09-25, doc 66: Sonnet 5 stays on its undated Nous id and a Sonnet snapshot swap is not detected — item 15); a model swap is a new trial and restarts that sleeve's paper evaluation. Roles rotate across providers daily; only market data and news leave the system, never account data or keys (*amended 2026-09-29, doc 66, #1881:* broker keys are trade-only, withdrawals disabled, IP-restricted where the venue offers it; a runtime guard refuses any LLM request carrying a known secret value before it is sent, and that name fails as an LLM error; secrets stay in gitignored files, no Keychain move); spend cap ~$30/month across providers, re-measured after paper week 1.

### 2.4 Validation, go-live, demotion

- **Q7 and Q19 — Gate.** (1) The backtest beats its benchmark after a 40% Sharpe haircut, DSR ≥ 0.95 deflated over every variant tried, PBO ≤ 0.10 (G9 confirms 0.10; the code bounds moved from 0.05 to 0.10 in Step 1, PR #1761), on 10+ years of history *(2026-10-05, doc 66, #1747: the haircut check must also hold with any one calendar year, the 2020 crash or the 2022 drawdown removed from the walk-forward path, Step 4b's regime split; a failure fails the candidate; item 25)* *(2026-10-07, doc 66, #1747: the haircut check must also hold with the signal one bar later, Step 4b's look-ahead canary; a failure fails the candidate; item 26)* *(2026-10-08, doc 66, #1747: the walk-forward edge over the benchmark must also sit above the 95th percentile of 200 matched random-entry runs; a failure fails the candidate; item 26)*. (2) 8–12 weeks of paper inside the backtest's 90% predictive band for the same window, with realised costs within ±25% of modelled (*2026-10-01, doc 66, #1884:* one Σ realised ÷ Σ modelled ratio per entry-offset group, against the simulator's pre-cost price on the same bar; slippage only on paper, fees join on live). (3) 4 consecutive weeks with zero plumbing faults (missed stop, reconcile mismatch, stuck order; *2026-09-30, doc 66:* also refused cycle, stale bar, failed broker call, missed run and token failure). (4) The G12 approval request. Start at the capital floor; ramp only while live stays in band.
- **Capital ceiling (Q7):** live capital ≤ £1,500 / (backtest max drawdown × 1.5).
- **G1 — Debate sleeve go-live.** Forward paper vs arm 2 with a pre-declared minimum of 100 closed trades and a one-sided test at 95%. Until met, its 30% stays in cash.
- **G7 — Demotion.** A live sleeve returns to paper when live return leaves the backtest's 95% band for 4 consecutive weeks, or drawdown exceeds 1.5× the backtest maximum.
- **Q15 — Backtest data.** Free data, survivorship-safe design. US: Alpaca free daily bars; US single-stock universe = point-in-time S&P 500 membership plus an explicit haircut for missing delisted names. Paid survivorship-free data only if the US single-stock part passes. ### 2.5 Self-improvement and autonomy

- **Q5 — Offline, gated.** A research loop learns from the trade journal and error log, proposes rule/parameter changes, walk-forward tests them under a global trial counter (DSR-deflated), and promotes via gate → paper fidelity → live. Live is frozen between promotions except for pre-declared, backtested adaptation rules (vol-scaling, trend filter, sleeve demotion) and risk that only tightens.
- **Q13 as amended by G12 — Authority.** Auto: research proposals, backtests, gate-passing promotion to paper, risk tightening. Anything reaching live (new sleeve, changed rule, capital increase) first passes the gate, then the system sends David an approval request on Telegram with a one-page summary (change, haircut backtest, paper fidelity, worst case vs £1,500). A "no" blocks it; no reply within 24 hours approves it, including for live money. Never: loosening the £1,500 or the daily cap mid-year.
- **G13 — Audit record and UI.** Every approval request, its reply or timeout, and its summary are written to a GitHub issue. There is no sign-off screen. The v2 dashboard's layout is rethought in the Step 3c spec (the v1 dashboard's v3 Rail layout, old ADR-0021, is not carried forward), and the dashboard is required before paper starts.
- **U1–U6 — Dashboard (doc 66, 2026-09-26).** One pause/halt control for all sleeves; resume never clears the loss-budget halt. The API is a v2 module started by `npm run v2:dashboard`, independent of v1's `service-api`. USD converts to GBP at the fixed 1 January rate. The gauge headline is the account-wide loss. Panels owned by later steps ship as not yet fed; the v1 client is replaced in the build. Manual pause/halt days count toward paper-band weeks only. Spec: `docs/specs/dashboard-spec.md`.
- **Q17 — Build order.** (1) momentum backtest and (2) debate audit in parallel, both £0 and LLM-free; (3) the v2 composition root with the Saxo simulated paper adapter and Alpaca paper, wired only for surviving sleeves; (4) protection — dead-man's switch, token refresh, loss-budget machinery — before any paper trade; (5) research loop last. Step 2 is done (doc 71) and its no-defect branch was ruled on 2026-09-22 — long and short, each side a counted trial vs arm 2 (§2.3), not short-only or veto-only.

### 2.7 Carried constraints (doc 66, not re-grilled)

- Every mandatory protective action is venue-resting or watchdog-backed, never tick-dependent (postmortem §3). A broker stop whose qty or price no longer matches the ledger after a split is cancelled and re-placed at once, accepting a window with no stop at the venue: one API round-trip when the cancel confirms, until the next run when it times out (a manual same-date re-run, otherwise the next scheduled weekday run); a failed re-place flattens at market with a critical alert, and entries stay blocked until reconcile is clean. A held broker position reconcile finds with no stop at all is re-armed the same way at its journalled stop (ruled 2026-10-02, doc 66, #1990). A date on which a run other than the one that marked it acted past its opening sweep is an explicit exception to replay determinism: the replay flags it `multiple_runs` for manual review (ruled 2026-10-02, doc 66, #1990). A replay whose inputs changed since the day (a journalled digest of the bar window the cycle read, or of the CFD catalogue, no longer matches) lists every changed input and exits with its own code, 2, apart from a divergence's 1; the digest covers only the window the cycle read, and a failed digest write is a fault that leaves the cycle's report standing (ruled 2026-10-05, doc 66, #2028).
- Every entry is journalled pending before it is sent to the venue, then settled to the venue's outcome; each run asks the venue for any order still pending, so a crash after the send leaves an id reconcile matches to the broker's order, and an order the venue never received resolves as a missed entry, never an open one (ruled 2026-10-04, doc 66, #1747).
- Windowed data reads carry tested coverage invariants (postmortem §2).
- Tax: per-disposal GBP conversion at the day's rate for US trades; W-8BEN. The live equity account is a Saxo GIA, so disposals are CGT events. The day's rate is the BoE XUDLUSS fix for the fill's date (ruled 2026-10-02, doc 66, #1947); its refresh is #2000.
- Paper profit is not evidence of edge; paper gates on fidelity to the backtest.

## 3. Definition of done for every step (doc 67 §5)

A step's PR ships its own unit tests, e2e tests where it touches a runtime path, passes oxlint + biome + fallow + the CRAP gate, and runs mutation testing on any risk, sizing or loss-budget code it adds. There is no separate testing phase; Step 4b's assurance checklist sits on top of this, not in place of it.

## 4. Still-true v1 decisions, restated

Originals at tag `v1-final` under `docs/adr/`.

- **Money-math precision (old ADR-0005):** money math stays float64 with a derived and measured error bound pinned as an executable test; no decimal library and no integer-minor-unit migration.
- **Daily equity return series (old ADR-0006):** the return series that metrics and kill lines read is an append-only table of one immutable portfolio-equity row per UTC day, sampled at the same session boundary the daily-loss breaker uses, never a widening of the breaker's own table.
- **Client/server layout and wire contracts (old ADR-0012):** `client/` (Vite) and `server/` (`apps/`, `pipeline/`, `providers/`, `shared/`, `tools/`) never import each other; both import `contracts/`, the JSON-serialisable wire model that neither owns; there is no root `src/`.
- **Dashboard hosting (old ADR-0019):** the dashboard stays co-located with the orchestrator, LAN-only, behind the fail-closed bind guard; no tunnel, no VPN overlay.

Old ADR-0021 (dashboard v3 rail layout) is deliberately not restated: G13 rethinks the layout.
